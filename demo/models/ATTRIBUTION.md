# Demo model attribution

`knee-bones.glb` is a subset of **Open3DModel – Lower limb – English labels**.
The original model was created by the Open3D project, Jan Kooloos,
Eungyeol Lee and the contributors credited on the source page. It is based
on BodyParts3D and Z-Anatomy. The 3D Anatomy project converted it to glTF.

- [Source model and contributor credits](https://ana3d.app/en/atlas/o3d-lower-limb/)
- [Open3DModel project](https://anatomytool.org/open3dmodel)
- [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

This adaptation selects four right-side bone meshes (femur, patella, tibia
and fibula) and repacks their glTF buffers. Geometry, normals, material
and model-space placement are preserved. The single-bone view shows the
femur mesh from the same file. The demo adds pin annotations.
